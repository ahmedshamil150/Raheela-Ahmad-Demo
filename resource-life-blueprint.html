Life Blueprint Assessment Questionnaire

Instructions: This questionnaire is designed to help you gain clarity about where you are today and where you want to go in the five key areas of life: Spirituality, Health, Relationships, Career, and Finances. Answer honestly. There are no right or wrong answers. The goal is awareness, clarity, and direction.

1. Spirituality

  1. What gives your life meaning and purpose?
  2. How connected do you currently feel to yourself, your values, or something greater than yourself?
  3. What practices help you feel centered, peaceful, or spiritually nourished?
  4. What inner struggle or spiritual challenge are you currently facing?
  5. If your spiritual life felt fully aligned, what would be different six months from now?

2. Health & Well-Being

  1. How would you rate your current physical and emotional health on a scale of 1?10?
  2. What health habit is helping you most right now?
  3. What health habit is holding you back the most?
  4. How does stress currently affect your body, emotions, or daily life?
  5. If your health improved significantly over the next year, what would that look like?

3. Relationships

  1. Which relationships currently bring you the most joy and support?
  2. Which relationships currently create stress, tension, or confusion?
  3. How comfortable are you expressing your needs, feelings, and boundaries?
  4. What relationship pattern would you like to improve or change?
  5. What would healthy, fulfilling relationships look like for you one year from now?

4. Career & Purpose

  1. What aspects of your work or daily activities energize and inspire you?
  2. What aspects of your work drain your energy?
  3. What skills, talents, or strengths do you feel are underutilized?
  4. If fear were not a factor, what career or professional goal would you pursue?
  5. Where would you ideally like your career or business to be three years from now?

5. Finances

  1. How satisfied are you with your current financial situation on a scale of 1?10?
  2. What are your biggest financial concerns right now?
  3. What financial habits are helping you move forward?
  4. What financial habits or beliefs may be holding you back?
  5. What does financial freedom mean to you personally?

Life Vision & Action Plan

  1. Which of the five life areas needs the most attention right now?
  2. Which area is currently your greatest strength?
  3. What is the biggest change you want to create in the next 12 months?
  4. What is one action you can take this week that would move you closer to that goal?
  5. Imagine your ideal life three years from now. What does it look and feel like?

Coach's Summary

After completing the questionnaire, identify:

  • Your top 3 strengths.
  • Your top 3 growth areas.
  • The biggest obstacle currently holding you back.
  • The most important goal for the next 12 months.
  • Three practical actions you will commit to taking in the next 30 days.
