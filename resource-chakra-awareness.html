Chakra Awareness Questionnaire

Find out which of your chakras are open, overactive, or need healing.

Instructions: Read each statement carefully and rate how true it feels for you right now: 5 ? Always true; 4 ? Often true; 3 ? Sometimes true; 2 ? Rarely true; 1 ? Never true

Root Chakra (Muladhara) ? Safety, Stability, Grounding

  • I feel safe and supported in my life.
  • I have a stable home and financial situation.
  • I trust that my basic needs will always be met.
  • I feel connected to my body and the physical world.
  • I can handle change without losing my balance.

Sacral Chakra (Svadhisthana) ? Emotions, Pleasure, Creativity

  • I easily express my emotions in healthy ways.
  • I enjoy my senses ? food, touch, art, and movement.
  • I feel comfortable with intimacy and connection.
  • I allow myself to play, imagine, and create.
  • I feel deserving of pleasure and joy.

Solar Plexus Chakra (Manipura) ? Confidence, Willpower, Personal Power

  • I believe in my ability to make things happen.
  • I set healthy boundaries with others.
  • I take responsibility for my actions and choices.
  • I feel confident in expressing my opinions.
  • I am motivated and can follow through on goals.

Heart Chakra (Anahata) ? Love, Compassion, Forgiveness

  • I forgive easily and let go of grudges.
  • I give and receive love with ease.
  • I feel empathy and compassion for myself and others.
  • I have healthy, nurturing relationships.
  • I allow vulnerability and connection.

Throat Chakra (Vishuddha) ? Expression, Truth, Communication

  • I speak my truth clearly and kindly.
  • I feel heard and respected in conversations.
  • I express myself creatively (writing, singing, art).
  • I can say 'no' when I need to.
  • I listen deeply to others.

Third Eye Chakra (Ajna) ? Intuition, Insight, Vision

  • I trust my inner voice and intuition.
  • I have a clear sense of direction and purpose.
  • I can visualize my goals vividly.
  • I?m open to spiritual insight and wisdom.
  • I trust that the universe guides me.

Crown Chakra (Sahasrara) ? Connection, Awareness, Spirituality

  • I feel connected to something greater than myself.
  • I experience moments of peace and unity.
  • I seek deeper understanding of life.
  • I feel guided or protected by divine energy.
  • I can detach from ego and trust the flow of life.

Scoring Summary

Add up each chakra's total (out of 25): 21?25: Chakra is open and balanced; 15?20: Chakra is somewhat balanced but needs gentle attention; 10?14: Chakra is underactive or blocked; Below 10: Chakra needs focused healing.
