FAQs

Your Questions, Gently Answered

It's normal to have questions before you begin. Here are the ones most people ask, thoughtfully and transparently answered.

What happens in the first consultation?

The first session is simply a conversation to get to know each other. We'll explore what brings you here, what you're hoping for, and whether we feel like the right fit. There is no pressure to commit to anything. By the end, you'll have a clear sense of next steps — whether that's continuing sessions, a recommendation for another practitioner, or simply time to decide.

How long is each session?

Most individual sessions are 50–60 minutes long. Initial consultations and couples sessions are typically 60–90 minutes. Reiki, hypnotherapy, and yoga sessions are usually 60 minutes. Numerology consultations are typically 60–90 minutes and include written notes afterwards.

How often will we meet?

This depends entirely on your needs and preferences. Most clients begin with weekly sessions, then gently space to fortnightly or monthly as they feel resourced enough. Some people come for a short course of 4–6 sessions; others stay for longer-term work. You are always in control of the pace.

How is my confidentiality protected?

Everything you share in session is held with the strictest confidence, in line with UK professional standards, GDPR, and clinical ethical practice. Notes are securely stored and password protected. The only rare exceptions to confidentiality are legal obligations such as risk of serious harm to yourself or others, or where a court order legally requires disclosure — and in such cases, you would always be consulted first where possible.

What is the difference between counseling, therapy, and psychotherapy?

These terms overlap significantly and are often used interchangeably. Generally speaking, "counseling" often refers to shorter-term, present-moment support with specific life challenges. "Psychotherapy" often refers to deeper, longer-term work exploring patterns, history, attachment, and identity. In practice, Raheelaa draws from both traditions according to what each person needs.

Do you work with specific cultural or religious backgrounds?

Absolutely. Raheelaa works with people of all cultural, ethnic, religious, spiritual, and secular backgrounds. She speaks English, Urdu, and Hindi fluently and has extensive experience supporting multicultural clients, Muslim and South Asian communities, and expats living abroad. Your identity, values, and beliefs are always respected and centered.

Is Reiki or Numerology the same as therapy?

No — this is an important distinction. Reiki, hypnotherapy (non-clinical uses), yoga & mindfulness, and numerology are offered as complementary wellness and self-discovery services. They are not a substitute for clinical psychotherapy or medical treatment. If you are unsure which service is most appropriate for your needs, please start with a consultation or psychotherapy — together we can discuss what would be most supportive and make appropriate recommendations.

How do online sessions actually work?

Online sessions are held via secure, encrypted video platforms. After booking, you'll receive a simple link via email — no software download required. Find a comfortable, private space where you won't be interrupted. Ensure your device is charged or plugged in, and ideally use headphones for better audio and privacy. Otherwise, it's exactly the same experience as sitting together in the room.

What are your fees and payment options?

Fees vary slightly depending on the service format (in-person vs online) and session type. Current fee information is shared during initial booking inquiry. Payment is accepted by bank transfer, secure card payment, PayPal, or Stripe, and is typically requested in advance of each session. A limited number of reduced-fee sliding-scale spots are sometimes available for those in genuine financial need — please ask if this applies to you.

What is your cancellation policy?

At least 48 hours notice is appreciated for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations with less than 48 hours notice may be charged in full. Emergencies and sudden illness are always handled with compassion and flexibility — please just let us know as soon as you are able.

How do I know if therapy is "working"?

Healing is rarely linear. It's normal to have sessions that feel challenging. Common early signs of progress include: feeling less alone in what you're carrying, small shifts in how you respond to triggers, noticing patterns you didn't see before, sleeping slightly better, or feeling a little kinder toward yourself. We'll periodically check in together about what you're noticing and what feels meaningful to you.

I'm still not sure which service is right. Where should I start?

You don't have to know. Most people don't. Simply book an initial consultation and mention "not sure yet" in your booking note. The initial session is designed exactly for this — to listen, understand what you're going through, and recommend the most appropriate service or combination of approaches. There is never any pressure to commit.
