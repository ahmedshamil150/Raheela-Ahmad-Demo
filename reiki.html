What is Reiki?

Reiki (pronounced Ray-key) is a Japanese name consisting of 2 words Rei & Ki meaning spiritually guided life energy, commonly known as “Universal Life Energy” — an energy which animates us all and is found all around us.

Reiki is a form of spiritual healing using “universal life energy” channelled through the practitioner to the recipient.

Reiki helps to harmonise body, mind and spirit for yourself or anyone you want to help.

Reiki is a simple, natural and safe method of spiritual healing and self-improvement that everyone can learn and use.

Who Can Learn?

Anyone can learn Reiki, irrespective of age, gender or religion. It is very easy and simple to learn, and anyone can apply it very effectively.

Treatment

In this treatment the recipient lies down or is seated comfortably, fully clothed, and the healer places their hands above or gently on the body. The treatment lasts for an hour or more depending on the nature of the condition. There are specific hand positions involved; it is a very soothing and relaxing experience and most people fall asleep during the treatment.
