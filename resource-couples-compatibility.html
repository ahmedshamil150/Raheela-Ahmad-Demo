Compatibility Questionnaire for Couples

Instructions: Each partner answers separately first and then discusses their answers together.

  1. What are the three most important values you want your relationship to be built on?
  2. When you are emotionally upset, what do you need most from your partner?
  3. What makes you feel deeply loved and appreciated?
  4. How do you usually react during conflict? Do you withdraw? Become emotional? Become defensive? Want immediate resolution? Need space first?
  5. What are your expectations regarding trust and loyalty in a relationship?
  6. What role should family members have in your relationship and major life decisions?
  7. How important are financial stability, lifestyle, and career ambitions to you?
  8. What does a healthy balance between togetherness and personal space look like for you?
  9. What are your biggest fears when it comes to relationships?
  10. How do you prefer to express love? Words, Physical affection, Quality time, Acts of service, Gifts, Emotional support
  11. What are three things that help you feel emotionally safe with someone?
  12. How do you imagine your life together five years from now?
  13. What personal qualities do you most admire in a life partner?
  14. What unresolved issue from your past do you think still affects your relationships today?
  15. When do you feel most connected, attracted, or drawn toward your partner?

Deeper Questions (Optional)

  1. What message about love or relationships did you learn growing up?
  2. What is something you wish people understood about you emotionally?
  3. What is one dream or life goal that is non-negotiable for you?
  4. How do you want to be treated when you are struggling emotionally?
  5. What does commitment mean to you?

Compatibility Reflection

After answering, discuss:

  • Where are our values similar?
  • Where are our expectations different?
  • What differences feel manageable?
  • What differences may create long-term challenges?
  • What strengths do we already have as a couple?
