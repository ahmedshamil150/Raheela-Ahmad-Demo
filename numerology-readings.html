Numerology

Discover the hidden meanings of numbers

Have you ever wondered what your date of birth reveals about you?

Numerology is a powerful tool of divination and self-discovery. By working with the numbers in your birth date and name, it can offer insight into personality, relationships, opportunities, timing and recurring patterns — helping you understand yourself and make more conscious choices.

What Can Numerology Help With?

  • Are you at a crossroads and trying to decide which career, business, country or major life direction is right for you?
  • Are you expecting a baby and want guidance in choosing a meaningful, numerologically supportive name?
  • Do you keep experiencing the same patterns in relationships, misunderstandings or break-ups?
  • Are you working hard but feel that opportunities and recognition keep passing you by?
  • Is your child struggling with focus, concentration or studies, and would you like another perspective on their natural tendencies?
  • Are you getting married and want to explore compatibility before making an important lifelong decision?
  • Are you already married and facing recurring difficulties despite trying everything you can?

If you are seeking answers to these questions or there are other areas of life where you need guidance, a numerology consultation is a right fit for you.

What Does a Numerology Analysis Include:

  • Clarity for the Questions You’re Asking
  • Is the vibration of your name and date of birth aligned or is it pulling you in opposite directions
  • Your potential, life purpose, strengths, weaknesses and challenges
  • Your hearts desire
  • Career choices
  • Lucky date, lucky number, lucky stones & lucky colors
