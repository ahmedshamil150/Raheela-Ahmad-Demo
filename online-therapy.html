How It Works

The Same Warmth, From a Distance

Many people worry that online sessions can't feel as deep, personal, or healing as meeting in person. The clients I work with online are often surprised — something sacred can still happen through a screen when the connection is genuine and the space is held well.

Online sessions are offered via secure, encrypted video platforms. All you need is a quiet private space, a stable internet connection, and the willingness to show up.

All the same services are available online — psychotherapy, trauma recovery, couples counseling, Reiki (distance healing), hypnotherapy, yoga & mindfulness, and numerology guidance.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions About Online Therapy

Is online therapy as effective as in-person?

Research consistently shows that online therapy is comparable in effectiveness to in-person therapy for most common concerns including anxiety, depression, PTSD, and relationship issues. Many clients actually prefer the online format.

What technology do I need?

A laptop, tablet, or smartphone with a camera, microphone, and stable internet connection is all you need. Sessions are held on secure encrypted video platforms with no software download required.

Is distance Reiki really possible?

Yes — Reiki and many energy healing traditions have long included distance or "absent" healing practice. Many clients report profound relaxation, release, and clarity from distance sessions, which are held with the same intention and presence as in-person Reiki.

What about time zones?

We work with clients across multiple continents and time zones. The booking system shows availability in your local timezone, and we aim to accommodate wherever we reasonably can.
