About Raheelaa

Meet Dr. Raheelaa Ahmad

"Healing is not simply about overcoming pain. It is about rediscovering the strength, clarity, and peace that already exist within you."

Dr. Raheelaa Ahmad is a UK-qualified psychotherapist and holistic healing practitioner who integrates modern psychotherapy with mind-body approaches including Reiki, mindfulness, yoga, hypnotherapy, and somatic healing.

Her mission is to create a safe and compassionate space where individuals can heal emotional wounds, overcome limiting patterns, and reconnect with their authentic selves. Whether you're struggling with anxiety, trauma, grief, relationships, or emotional burnout, each session is thoughtfully tailored to your personal journey.
