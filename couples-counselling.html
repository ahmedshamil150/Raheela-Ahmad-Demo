COUPLE THERAPY

Relationships can be deeply fulfilling, but they also go through challenging phases. Misunderstandings, communication gaps, unresolved emotions, or differences in expectations can sometimes create distance between two people who care for each other. Couples therapy provides a safe, supportive, and non-judgmental space where both partners can come together, be heard, and understand each other better. It is not about deciding who is right or wrong, or taking sides. It's about understanding the relationship as a whole and exploring communication patterns, emotional needs, expectations, recurring conflicts, and unresolved hurts.

During sessions, both partners have the opportunity to express their feelings and concerns in a structured and respectful environment, while learning to listen and communicate without blame or defensiveness. With guidance and practical tools, couples can learn to communicate more openly, understand each other's needs and perspectives, break unhealthy patterns of conflict, rebuild emotional connection and trust, and develop healthier ways of resolving disagreements.

You don't need to have everything figured out before starting. Therapy offers a space to slow down, understand what's happening beneath the conflict, and begin finding a way forward together.

My Approach

Over the last seven years, while working with couples, I've realized what couples need most is being heard and understood. This is why, in my couples therapy sessions, I create a safe and supportive space where both partners can express themselves openly and feel genuinely heard.

Alongside this, I help couples set meaningful goals, identify their individual needs and value systems, and work towards aligning them in a way that feels right for both partners.

In addition to my training in couples counselling, I also incorporate NLP techniques to help release negative patterns from the past. These techniques can help work through old resentments, let go of patterns affecting the relationship, and create space for something new, loving, peaceful, and warm.

If you feel that you and your partner are going through a turbulent relationship or if you're looking to get married and want to start a wonderful life together, I would strongly recommend to get a premarital counselling.
