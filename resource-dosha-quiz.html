Ayurvedic Dosha Self-Assessment (Vata, Pitta, Kapha)

For each statement, choose the option that fits you best: A = Vata | B = Pitta | C = Kapha

Assessment

  1. My body frame is: A) Thin/light B) Medium/athletic C) Large/solid
  2. My skin is: A) Dry/rough B) Warm/oily C) Smooth/moist
  3. My appetite is: A) Irregular B) Strong C) Steady/slow
  4. My energy levels are: A) Variable B) Intense C) Stable
  5. My temperament is: A) Anxious/creative B) Driven/irritable C) Calm/patient
  6. My sleep is: A) Light/interrupted B) Moderate C) Deep/long
  7. My digestion is: A) Irregular B) Fast C) Slow
  8. My reaction to stress: A) Worry B) Anger C) Withdrawal
  9. My speech is: A) Fast B) Sharp C) Slow/steady
  10. My memory is: A) Quick to learn, quick to forget B) Sharp C) Slow but long-lasting

Scoring Method

Count how many A, B, and C answers you selected. A = Vata B = Pitta C = Kapha. Highest score = Dominant dosha. If two are close, you may have a dual dosha type (e.g., Vata-Pitta). If all are similar, you may be Tridoshic (balanced).

Dosha Interpretations

Vata: Creative, energetic, but prone to anxiety and imbalance.

Pitta: Focused, ambitious, but prone to irritability and burnout.

Kapha: Calm, stable, but prone to sluggishness and attachment.

Remedies & Balancing Tips

Vata: Warm foods, routine, grounding practices, gentle yoga.

Pitta: Cooling foods, relaxation, avoid overheating, meditation.

Kapha: Light foods, movement, stimulation, avoid stagnation.
