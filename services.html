Therapeutic Hypnotherapy

Helping clients reshape limiting beliefs, manage habits, reduce anxiety, and build healthier mental patterns using guided therapeutic techniques. Hypnotherapy works with the subconscious mind — the part of us where long-held beliefs, habits, and emotional patterns are stored.

Contrary to common myths, you remain fully awake and in control during hypnosis. The process is deeply relaxing, gentle, and collaborative. It is simply a state of focused, receptive attention where new suggestions can take root.

  • Limiting beliefs & inner critic work
  • Anxiety, phobias & panic response
  • Habit change (smoking, eating, sleep)
  • Self-confidence & self-esteem
  • Motivation & goal alignment
  • Past & inner-child regression (if appropriate)
  • Relaxation & stress response re-training
